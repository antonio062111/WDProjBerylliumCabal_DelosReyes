About Lakbay

Our mission and the developers of Lakbay


Our Mission

We at Lakbay aim to promote tourism in the Philippines, not just in the popular tourist destinations, but also in the less common regions of the country. Through this website we also aim to preserve the rich history of these "not so common" places in the country. Lastly, we would like to give the people of these regions a chance to showcase their hospitality and culture.



Empowering Local Tourism

We spotlight lesser-known destinations to help communities thrive through sustainable travel.

Preserving Culture

We share Filipino traditions, festivals, and crafts to keep heritage alive.

Building Connections

We believe that travelling is like a bridge between people, places, and stories that are worth sharing.
