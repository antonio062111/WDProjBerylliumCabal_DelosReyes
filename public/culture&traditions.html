Cultures & Traditions

Explore the rich cultural heritage and traditions of the Philippines

Five Cultural Highlights

Camiguin

Known as the "Island Born of Fire," Camiguin blends volcanic landscapes with traditions like the Lanzones Festival.

Romblon

Famous for marble crafts, Romblon preserves handcrafted traditions passed down through generations.

Siquijor

Known for mystical healing practices and folk traditions, Siquijor offers a unique cultural experience.

Tawi-Tawi

Rich in Islamic heritage, Tawi-Tawi showcases Sama-Bajau traditions and coastal community life.

Bukidnon

Home to the Kaamulan Festival, Bukidnon celebrates indigenous tribes through rituals, dances, and crafts.
