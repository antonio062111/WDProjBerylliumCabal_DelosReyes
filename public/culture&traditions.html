Cultures & Traditions

Explore the rich cultural heritage and traditions of the Philippines

Five Cultural Highlights

Siargao

Known as the surfing capital of the Philippines, Siargao also celebrates island traditions and coastal community life.

Vigan

Spanish colonial streets and ancestral houses, with cultural crafts like Abel Iloco weaving and pottery.

Batanes

Rolling hills, Ivatan stone houses, and traditions rooted in resilience and harmony with nature.

Quezon

Home to the Pahiyas Festival in Lucban, where houses are decorated with colorful kiping and harvest goods.

Aklan

Famous for the Ati-Atihan Festival in Kalibo, with tribal dances, costumes, and devotion to Santo Niño.
